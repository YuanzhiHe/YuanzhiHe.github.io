OptiVLA: Inference-Time Optimization for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Manipulation

AAMAS 2027 · Int. Conf. on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems
First authorCCF B
Overview

A two-stage VLA framework that keeps a frozen LLaVA-style VLM external to a point-cloud conditional-flow-matching policy, then at inference scores the candidate action chunks it proposes against a reusable task-space cost library and executes the lowest-cost one. Raises a strong base policy from 65.0% to 69.0% across six RLBench tasks under a matched three-seed protocol, and transfers zero-shot to a real Franka arm with 5/5 on each of three contact-rich tasks, where the base policy and a planner without learned proposals fail every trial.

VLA · Flow Matching · Best-of-K · RLBench · Franka FR3
