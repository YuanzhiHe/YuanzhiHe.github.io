about

Teaching robots to learn, sample‑efficiently.

PhD researcher fascinated by how robots can acquire dexterous skills with minimal data, by combining deep RL, evolutionary search and modern VLA models.

I'm Yuanzhi He, a fully‑funded PhD student at Cardiff University's School of Computer Science & Informatics. My research lives at the intersection of deep reinforcement learning, evolutionary strategies, flow matching, and vision‑language‑action models, applied to robotic manipulation on platforms like the Franka Emika FR3.

I'm especially interested in sample efficiency: how transfer learning, task decomposition and hierarchical control let real robots learn complex skills without million-step simulation budgets. I'm currently at the National Institute of Informatics (NII, Tokyo) as a research intern, working on sim‑to‑real transfer for dual‑arm VLA manipulation.

Outside the lab you'll find me on a badminton court, a squash court, on the bike, or sat across a Go board.

P2T · Direct-Torque Control for Quadrupedal Locomotion

PhD research · Cardiff University

Learned quadruped controllers almost always emit joint position targets that a fixed PD loop tracks, which caps command bandwidth. P2T learns a direct-torque policy in a single co-training stage from a low-gain PD position policy — no pre-trained teacher, no distillation. Under realistic 5–10 ms latency it retains 1.7× more high-frequency command authority, cuts the rough-terrain fall rate to under half of both position baselines, and runs on a physical Unitree Go2 at 100 Hz through the low-level torque interface.

Unitree Go2PPOTorque ControlIsaac SimSim-to-Real

OptiVLA · Optimization-in-the-Loop VLA Policies

PhD research · Cardiff University

A two-stage manipulation framework that keeps a frozen LLaVA-style VLM outside the action decoder: a zero-initialised projection grafts the language embedding onto a point-cloud conditional-flow-matching policy, then an improved Cross-Entropy Method refines the sampled action chunk at inference against a reusable task-space cost library. 66.3% → 68.8% across six RLBench tasks, 5/5 vs. 0/5 on three contact-rich tasks after zero-shot transfer to a real Franka Research 3, at roughly 18 Hz.
